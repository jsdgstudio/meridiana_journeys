# Task 07: About & Journal Pages

## Objective
Build the `/about` and `/journal` pages — supporting editorial pages.

## About Page (`app/about/page.tsx`)

### Sections:
1. **Hero** — Statement of purpose, image of the team/founder or a cultural moment
2. **Story** — Long-form narrative: why Meridiana exists, the philosophy
3. **Values** — 3 core values in editorial format (not icons + bullets)
4. **Team** — Optional placeholder for founder bio
5. **CTA** — "Let's plan your journey"

### Content file: `content/pages/about.json`
