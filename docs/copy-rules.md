# Copy Rules — The Voice of Meridiana

## Tone Profile

Meridiana speaks like: a well-traveled cultural journalist who writes for an intelligent, curious audience. Think long-form pieces in *Monocle*, *Condé Nast Traveler* (the essays, not the listicles), or *Afar*.

### Voice Attributes
- **Literary** — prose-forward, not marketing-forward
- **Informed** — culturally specific, never surface-level
- **Measured** — confident without being loud
- **Warm** — approachable depth, not academic distance
- **Cinematic** — visual language, sensory pacing

---

## The Golden Rules

### 1. Narrative must remain intact
Never flatten long-form storytelling into bullet points. If a paragraph exists, it exists for a reason. Edit for clarity, never for brevity at the expense of depth.

### 2. No tourism clichés
These phrases are banned:

| ❌ Banned | ✅ Alternative |
|-----------|---------------|
| Hidden gem | A place most visitors never find |
| Off the beaten path | Beyond the familiar routes |
| Experience the magic | (Just describe what makes it remarkable) |
| Discover paradise | (Be specific about what they'll find) |
| Immerse yourself | Step into / Walk through / Enter |
| Breathtaking views | (Describe the actual view) |
| Once-in-a-lifetime | (Prove it through detail, don't claim it) |
| Authentic experience | (Authenticity is shown, not declared) |
| Local flavor | (Name the actual food, tradition, place) |
| Unforgettable | (Make them remember by writing well) |

### 3. Specificity over superlatives
- ❌ "The best coffee in Colombia"
- ✅ "A single-origin Geisha from a family finca in Huila, processed at 1,800 meters"

### 4. Show cultural knowledge
- Reference historical context
- Name specific neighborhoods, artisans, traditions
- Connect past and present
- Acknowledge complexity (Colombia is not a postcard)

### 5. Respect the reader
- Assume intelligence and curiosity
- Don't over-explain
- Trust them to engage with density
- No patronizing "fun facts" tone

## Tour Naming Convention

### Structure: `[Anchor] — [Editorial Subtitle]`

| ❌ Generic | ✅ Meridiana |
|------------|-------------|
| Bogotá City Tour | Classic Bogotá — Five Centuries in Three Days |
| Coffee Region Trip | Eje Cafetero — From Seed to Summit |
| Cartagena Beach Tour | Cartagena Unveiled — Beyond the Fortress Walls |
| Amazon Jungle Tour | Leticia & the Amazon — Where the River Begins |

## Section-Specific Guidelines

### Hero Headlines
- Max 8 words
- Evocative, not descriptive
- Can be a fragment (no verb required)
- Must work over an image

### Card Descriptions (narrative.short)
- 2–3 sentences
- Must stand alone (no "click to learn more")
- Hook + context + intrigue
- Written in present tense

### Long-form Narrative (narrative.long)
- 300–600 words
- Opening paragraph must be compelling (this is your lede)
- Middle builds context and cultural depth
- Closing creates desire without selling
- Can include a pull quote for visual rhythm

### CTA Copy
- Warm, invitational tone
- ❌ "Book Now!" / "Don't Miss Out!"
- ✅ "Explore This Journey" / "Request Your Itinerary" / "Begin Planning"

### Metadata/Labels
- Duration: "3 days / 2 nights" (not "3D/2N")
- Group size: "2–8 travelers" (not "pax" or "guests")
- Price: "From $X per person" (transparent, no asterisks)

---

## Language & Grammar

- American English spelling
- Oxford comma: yes
- Em dashes with no spaces: word—word
- Numbers: spell out one through nine, numerals for 10+
- Dates: "March 15, 2025" format
- Times: "9:00 a.m." format
